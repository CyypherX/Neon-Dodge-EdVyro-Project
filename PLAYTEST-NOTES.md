## Before → After

**Before:** Core state/progression/persistence build with basic gameplay feedback.

**After:** Added event-based audio feedback, mute control, reduced-motion option, visible focus styles, live accessibility announcements, and structured playtesting documentation. Playtesting also identified the need for stronger and more satisfying sound effects.

## Highest-impact finding

The highest-impact recurring finding was the need for more satisfying and varied sound effects. Two of the three playtesters specifically requested additional or more satisfying audio feedback.

**Change implemented:** Expanded audio feedback for important gameplay events and interactions so that actions such as obstacle destruction and progression provide clearer and more satisfying feedback without obscuring gameplay.
